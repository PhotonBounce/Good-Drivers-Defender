## Overview
Good Drivers Defender ("the app") is a dashcam and trip-recording app. We designed it to keep your data on
your device. This policy explains what the app accesses, why, and your choices.

## Information the app accesses
- **Camera (video):** to record dashcam footage. Recording only occurs when you start it.
- **Microphone (audio):** to record audio alongside video.
- **Location (GPS, precise/approximate):** to log your route, speed, and incident locations while a
  recording session (foreground service) is active.
- **Storage / media:** to save recordings and exported reports on your device.
- **Advertising identifier:** the Google AdMob SDK accesses your device's advertising ID to serve and
  measure ads (for non-paying users).

## How information is used
- Recordings, location logs, and incident records are stored **locally on your device**. The app does not
  upload them to our servers (we do not operate servers for your content).
- You control sharing: footage or reports leave your device only when **you** explicitly export or share them.
- Advertising data is processed by Google to provide ads. See Google's policies below.

## Data sharing
- We do **not** sell your personal data.
- The only third party that receives data is **Google AdMob** (advertising identifier and standard ad
  request data), and only for users who have not purchased the ad-free upgrade.

## Data retention and deletion
- Your recordings and logs remain on your device until you delete them in-app or uninstall the app.
- Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data.

## Purchases
- The optional "Pro" subscription is processed by **Google Play Billing**. We do not receive or store your
  payment details.

## Permissions you control
You can grant or revoke camera, microphone, and location permissions at any time in your device's Settings.
Revoking required permissions will disable the corresponding recording features.

## Children
The app is intended for licensed drivers and is **not directed to children**. We do not knowingly collect
data from children.

## Audio/recording laws
Recording laws (including audio consent) vary by jurisdiction. You are responsible for using the app in
compliance with the laws that apply to you.
