## Full description (≤4000 chars)

```
Good Drivers Defender turns your Android phone into a professional dashcam and driving-safety recorder — no extra hardware needed.

── WHAT IT DOES ──────────────────────────────────
Start a recording session and the app watches your drive. GPS-stamped incident records, timestamped snapshots and video clips, and ambient audio are saved directly on your device so you always have a clear, timestamped evidence log of what happened on the road.

── KEY FEATURES ──────────────────────────────────

📹 DASHCAM RECORDING
Continuous video + timestamped snapshots with GPS overlay. Auto-starts when you move, auto-stops after parking.

💥 COLLISION DETECTION
The accelerometer watches for sudden 3.5 G impacts. A full-screen crash alert fires instantly with a countdown-to-auto-alert and I'M OK / SEND HELP buttons.

🆘 EMERGENCY SOS
Arm with one tap. A 3-2-1 countdown shares your live GPS location and last recorded incident summary to emergency contacts.

🏆 DRIVE SCORE
Gamified 0–100 safety score with a letter grade, XP system, driver level, and 6 unlockable achievement badges — all calculated from your actual driving record.

🛡️ PARKING SENTRY
Radar-sweep guard mode detects bumps while your car is parked and logs each impact with timestamp and G-force severity.

🔭 AR HEADS-UP DISPLAY
Windshield-projection HUD with a tilting artificial horizon, giant speed readout, live G-force meter, and mirror-flip mode for phone-on-dash mounting.

📊 LIVE TELEMETRY GRAPH
Real-time dual-line chart of speed and G-force with NOW / MAX / AVG / PEAK statistics.

📋 EVIDENCE TIMELINE
Color-coded incident log sorted by severity (green / orange / red by G-force). Tap any event to review GPS, speed, media, and notes.

🚗 TRIP HISTORY
Past drive sessions grouped automatically from your incident log — each with a speed sparkline and summary stats.

📦 EVIDENCE BUNDLE EXPORT
One tap compiles a timestamped ZIP containing a written report, photos, video clips, witness audio, and GPS telemetry CSV — ready to attach to an email or filing.

── YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE ────────────────
Recordings, GPS logs, and incident records are stored locally. Nothing is uploaded to any server. You control what you share, when, and with whom.

── FREE vs PRO ───────────────────────────────────
Free: dashcam recording, basic incident logging (3/day), GPS tagging, evidence timeline, drive score.
Defender Pro (subscription): unlimited incidents, unlimited trip length, Collision Detection, Emergency SOS, Parking Sentry, AR HUD, Live Telemetry, Evidence ZIP export, and Achievement badges. Every new install starts with a 7-day free VIP trial — no ads, ever.

── PERMISSIONS ───────────────────────────────────
Camera, Microphone, and Location are required for the dashcam to function. A notification is shown whenever background recording is active so you always know it's running.

Note: Audio recording laws vary by location. You are responsible for complying with local consent requirements.

Privacy Policy: https://photon-bounce.com/privacy.html
```
