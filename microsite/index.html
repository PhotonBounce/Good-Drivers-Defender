How it works

Protection in three taps

No account, no setup wizard, no cloud. Mount your phone and drive.

1

Start a trip

Tap START TRIP — or let auto-motion detection arm recording the moment you pull away. Road video, audio, GPS and g-force begin logging instantly.

2

Drive — it watches

The risk halo reads speed, braking and cornering in real time. Tap LOG INCIDENT to bookmark a moment, or let collision detection flag hard impacts for you.

3

Review & export

Get a Drive Score with coaching, browse your evidence locker, and export a timestamped ZIP bundle — all stored privately on your device.

Questions

Good to know

When is it launching?

It's in final pre-launch testing and coming soon to Google Play. This page is a preview of what's shipping.

Does it really work without the internet?

Yes. Recording, scoring, GPS logging and evidence storage are 100% on-device. The only network use is Google Play for the subscription.

Are there ads?

Never. The app is subscription-only with a generous free tier — no advertising SDK and no advertising ID.

How does the free trial work?

Every new install gets full VIP/Pro access free for 7 days. After that you keep a capable free tier, or subscribe to stay VIP.

Where is my footage stored?

In the app's private storage on your device, and excluded from Google cloud backup. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones (Android 8.0 and up). A mount with a clear view of the road works best.
