Everything you need on the road

📹

Dashcam Recording

Continuous video + timestamped snapshots with GPS overlay. Auto-starts when you move, auto-stops when you park.

🆘

Emergency SOS

One tap arms a 3-2-1 countdown that texts your live GPS and last-incident summary to emergency contacts.

💥

Collision Detection

Accelerometer watches for 3.5 G impact. Instant full-screen crash alert with auto-dispatch countdown.

🏆

Drive Score

Gamified 0–100 safety score with letter grade, XP, driver level, and unlockable achievement badges.

🛡️

Parking Sentry

Radar-sweep guard mode. Detects bumps while parked and logs them with timestamp and G-force severity.

🔭

AR Heads-Up Display

Windshield-projection HUD with tilting artificial horizon, giant speed readout, and mirror-flip mode.

📋

Evidence Timeline

Color-coded incident log sorted by severity. Tap any event to review GPS, speed, G-force, and media.

📊

Live Telemetry

Real-time dual-line chart of speed and G-force with NOW / MAX / AVG / PEAK statistics.

📦

Evidence Bundle Export

One tap creates a timestamped ZIP with report, photos, video clips, audio, and GPS telemetry CSV.
