Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  App: Good Drivers Defender (v2.0)

2. What data the app collects

Good Drivers Defender collects the following data when you use the app:

  • Precise location (GPS) — recorded at each incident and during active trip sessions to geotag evidence and log driving speed.
  • Camera (video and photos) — captured during dashcam recording and at the moment you log an incident. Used solely for on-device evidence storage.
  • Microphone (audio) — recorded as ambient audio during active trip sessions. Stored on-device alongside video evidence.
  • Accelerometer (G-force) — sampled continuously while the app is open to detect hard-braking events and collisions. This is sensor data; it is never uploaded.
  • Advertising ID — used by Google AdMob (for free-tier users only) to serve relevant ads. See Section 5.

3. How data is stored

All recordings, GPS logs, and incident records are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage or in your Downloads folder (when you export). Nothing is uploaded to our servers or any third-party server, with the exception of the advertising SDK described in Section 5.

4. How data is shared

We do not sell, rent, or share your recordings, location history, or incident data with any third party. The only sharing is:

  • At your choice — when you tap "Share" or "Export" within the app, your device's system share sheet sends the file to an app of your choosing (email, messaging, etc.).
  • Google AdMob — see Section 5.

5. Advertising (free tier)

Free-tier users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID and device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google Settings → Ads. Upgrading to Defender Pro disables all ads.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Permissions explained

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — required to log GPS coordinates and speed at each incident. Active only while the foreground service is running.
  • Camera (CAMERA) — required to record dashcam video and take evidence photos.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — required to record ambient audio as part of the evidence session.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — required on Android 13+ to show the ongoing notification that indicates active background recording. You will always know when the app is recording.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — used on older Android versions to save evidence files to public Downloads.
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK) — keeps the processor active during a recording session so no frames are missed.

7. Audio recording notice

This app can record audio from your device's microphone during active sessions. Audio recording laws vary by jurisdiction. You are responsible for ensuring you comply with all applicable local laws regarding audio recording, including any consent requirements for recording passengers or other parties.

8. Data retention and deletion

All incident records, GPS tracks, photos, videos, and audio files are stored locally on your device. You can delete individual incidents from within the app (Evidence Locker → swipe to delete). Uninstalling the app removes all data stored in the app's private storage. Files you exported to your Downloads folder must be deleted manually.

9. Children's privacy

Good Drivers Defender is intended for use by adults (drivers). It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
